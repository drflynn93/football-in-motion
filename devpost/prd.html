Before playback

Bills vs Chiefs · 1/23/2022

Play

The viewer chooses when to begin. Pressing Play reveals the horizontal measurement scale, then the vertical scale, then the team names beside the vertical axis.

During the game

A scrolling play-by-play ticker sits above the chart. Coin-toss context opens the story if verified. Paths evolve with each play; small team names follow their growing tips. At the starting point, Bills is just left of center and Chiefs just right.

Pause / resumePlayback speedAbout 7 minutes at normal speed

Key-event thought bubbles appear briefly, disappear, and leave yellow dots. Hover to read the information again and pause; leave to resume at the same moment.

After the final play

The completed chart remains visible. Final score: Bills 36 · Chiefs 42. A box score appears below the horizontal axis and a Replay button starts the full game again.

Passing and rushing yardage entries are clickable. Both teams have rows for passing yards, rushing yards, total return yards, interceptions made, and opponent fumbles recovered. Return yards combine PR/KR/IR/FR; interceptions and recoveries are counts, including zero.

After clicking passing or rushing yards

A second chart animates below the main chart. Its horizontal dimension is game time; its vertical dimension is the selected type of yards. Little markers show quarter-end totals.

A team selector offers Bills, Chiefs, or Compare both for the selected statistic. It first shows the winning team (Chiefs), with Compare both visible. The approved lower chart uses elapsed game time with quarter-end and overtime-ending markers.

Main chart: score across, yards upward

No score change means no horizontal movement. Yardage gains move up; losses move down. Both teams start at the bottom center. Matching scores are equally far from center on opposite sides; these are actual point values, not negative scores.

The full chart will have a numbered yardage scale. At equal yardage, horizontal separation equals the two scores added together, not the scoring margin; the labels must make each team's score clear.

October 5 amendment · Approved

Fourth-down decisions

Use the same Bills / Chiefs / Compare both selector, initially showing Chiefs. In comparison mode, keep separate summaries for each team.

SummaryMeaning
Number of fourth downsClick to open the decision bar chart.
Go-for-it count and %Attempts divided by all fourth-down decisions.
Conversions and success %Conversions divided by go-for-it attempts. Example only: 3 / 4 = 75%.

The decision bar chart

One bar per fourth-down decision. Height is yards needed, not yards gained.

Green · went for it and convertedRed · went for it and failedGray · puntGold · made field goalRed/gray · missed field goal

Approved fourth-down details
  • Order the bars chronologically, with team and game-clock identification.
  • Hover for exact yards needed, decision, and result.
  • Show N/A for conversion success when there are no go-for-it attempts.
  • Show a zero count and a no-bars message when there are no fourth-down decisions.
  • Label fourth-and-goal distances as goal-to-go.

This describes the decisions teams made; it does not claim to calculate the optimal choice. Fetching and adding games, including high school games, remain later enhancements.

Teaching takeaway: fourth-down frequency, willingness to go for it, and conversion success have different denominators. Keeping them distinct prevents misleading percentages.

What we will check on the screen
  • The opening title, date and Play button are present.
  • If game data cannot be displayed, the matchup remains visible with an explanatory message and a Retry button.
  • Axes reveal in the agreed order, and both names follow their paths.
  • Scoring moves Bills left and Chiefs right; yardage changes height.
  • Pause holds the current state and speed control changes the rate.
  • Hover pauses and restores event information; leaving resumes without resetting.
  • The normal-speed game takes about seven minutes excluding pauses.
  • The replay includes overtime and ends with Bills 36, Chiefs 42.
  • The box score and Replay control appear at the end. Both teams show passing yards, rushing yards, total return yards, interceptions made, and opponent fumbles recovered.
  • A selected-stat animation appears below, with quarter-total markers and a Bills / Chiefs / Compare both selector. Chiefs appear first and Compare both is visible.
